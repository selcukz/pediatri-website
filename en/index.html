About

Every visit begins with a conversation.

For international families

New to Turkey? You're in good hands.

Practical answers for expat parents — language, appointments, vaccination records from abroad and insurance.

Are consultations available in English?

Yes. Dr. Zeybek sees patients in both English and Turkish, and consultations for international families are carried out in English — including explanations, follow-up plans and answers to your questions.

How do I book an appointment?

Appointments are booked through Koç University Hospital's official system: online via the hospital's English booking page or by calling the call centre at +90 850 250 8 250. Choose Pediatrics (Child Health and Diseases) and select Dr. Aslı Ece Zeybek.

Where is the clinic located?

At Koç University Hospital in Istanbul — a private university hospital. The hospital's website (kuh.ku.edu.tr/en) has directions and visitor information in English.

My child started their vaccinations in another country. Can we continue in Turkey?

Yes. Bring your child's vaccination records to the visit; the schedule your child started abroad is reviewed and harmonised with the Turkish national immunisation schedule, so no doses are missed or repeated unnecessarily.

Does the hospital work with private or international health insurance?

Koç University Hospital is a private university hospital and works with many insurance providers. Coverage depends on your policy — the hospital's international patient services (kochealthcare.org) can confirm whether your insurance is accepted before your visit.
